Top recommendation

1. Collapse the earn-scope triplication — then 2. Deepen the inflation clamp.

Scope triplication is the live drift risk: backfill must match the award path commit-for-commit. Clamp drift (earn partial-credit vs redeem all-or-nothing) is the same shape one seam over. Fixing both puts the two money-moving decisions behind tested interfaces.
